Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Snipster runs on your own computer. We do not run servers that collect your videos or personal data, and we do not sell or share your data.

What data Snipster handles

  • Your videos and clips are processed and stored locally on your computer.
  • TikTok account data: when you connect TikTok, Snipster receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, username, avatar) via TikTok's Login Kit, plus the posting options TikTok allows for your account. This is used only to show which account is connected and to upload the clips you choose.
  • API keys you enter for AI services are stored in the macOS Keychain on your computer.

How data is stored

TikTok tokens and API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain on your device. They are never sent to us.

What is sent to third parties

  • TikTok: only the clip, caption and settings you choose to upload, when you click the upload button.
  • AI services (if you enable them): video transcripts or text needed to find highlights and write captions, sent with your own API key and subject to that provider's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Click "Disconnect" in Snipster to remove your TikTok token from your computer. You can also revoke Snipster's access in your TikTok app settings. Deleting the Snipster folder removes all local clips.

Children

Snipster is not directed at children under 13.
